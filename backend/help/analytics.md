# Community Analytics

Understanding your community metrics

Community Analytics gives you a real-time view of each client’s community health. The Overview aggregates every connected platform; each platform you connect also appears automatically as its own entry under Community Analytics.

## How to use it
1. Open "Overview" for the aggregated dashboard across all platforms, with dynamic Platform / Metric / Range filters.
2. Each connected platform (Discord, Telegram, Zealy, Galxe…) shows up on its own under Community Analytics, no setup, it appears the moment you connect it in Integrations.
3. Windows adapt to the data you actually hold: 1h / 5h use hourly snapshots, 24h / 7d / 30d use the daily rollup.

## Metrics explained
- **Active Members**, Unique users who sent at least one message in the selected period. Counted from platform API data (Discord + Telegram combined). Primary indicator of real community engagement vs. passive membership.
- **Messages This Week**, Total messages sent across connected platforms. Summed from Discord channel messages and Telegram group messages. Measures conversation volume and content activity level.
- **New Members**, Net new joins in the period. Join events tracked via platform webhooks and API. Measures community growth rate and acquisition effectiveness.
- **Retention & tenure**, How long members stay, and how many recent joiners are still around. Built from Discord member tenure + membership snapshots over time. Growth means little if members churn, retention shows real stickiness.
- **Period-over-period %**, Comparison vs. the previous equivalent period. Current period value compared against the prior period of equal length. Green means improvement, red means decline, quick health signal at a glance.

## Pro tips
- A sudden drop in active members often signals a bad news event, investigate immediately.
- Spikes in scam alerts often precede market volatility, treat them as an early warning.
- Share analytics screenshots directly in client reports for extra credibility.
