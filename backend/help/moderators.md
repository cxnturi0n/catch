# Moderators

Your team hub, directory, analytics, payments, reports, leaderboard

Everything about your team lives here, split into tabs: Directory, Analytics, Payments, Reports and Leaderboard. Under the Team group alongside KOLs.

## How to use it
1. Directory: add moderators with handles, timezone and shift, and link their Discord or Telegram user from the member picker so activity follows the person; click a row to open the full profile (bio, skills, CV).
2. Analytics: performance, punctuality vs assigned shifts, response rate, and the activity heatmap for planning coverage.
3. Payments: set point values per metric and a points→currency rate, then track earnings and the salary log.
4. Reports: per-moderator drill-downs. Leaderboard: your most active contributors at a glance.

## Metrics explained
- **Shift Coverage %**, Shifts completed vs. shifts assigned. Completed shifts divided by assigned shifts, as a percentage. Below 80% requires attention, it signals scheduling or reliability problems.
- **Avg Response Time**, How quickly the moderator answers members. Per channel, the first moderator message within 60 minutes after a member message counts as its answer (a reply targets that message). Computed nightly from collected messages. Target under 5 minutes.
- **Bans Executed**, Total bans and kicks performed by the moderator (timeouts and mutes shown next to it). Discord audit log (the bot needs View Audit Log) and Telegram admin actions, attributed through the linked platform user id or the handle. Shows enforcement activity and workload distribution across the team.
- **Rating**, Overall performance score for the moderator. Auto-calculated from coverage rate and warning count. 5.0 is a perfect score, use it to spot who needs support or recognition.

## Pro tips
- Always build overlap between shifts so handoffs don’t drop context.
- Night shift is hardest to fill, consider volunteers in Asian timezones.
