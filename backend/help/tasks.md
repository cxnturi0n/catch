# Task Manager

Table, board and time-blocked calendar in one place

Plan and track work across your team from one place — as a table, a board, or a time-blocked calendar.

## How to use it
1. The default table has Area, To do, Status, Assignee, Start date and Due date — click a column header arrow to sort by it.
2. Assign work to yourself or anyone on the team — assignees are not limited to moderators.
3. Switch to Board or Calendar, or drag the divider between the two panes for an adjustable split view.
4. The calendar is time-blocked in 30-minute slots; use the filter to show Tasks, Meetings, or both.

## Views explained
- **Table** — Sortable columns (Area, To do, Status, Assignee, Start, Due) — the fastest way to scan everything.
- **Board** — Kanban columns by status — drag work forward as it progresses.
- **Calendar** — Day/week grid in 30-minute slots. Tasks and meetings appear at their times; filter between them.
- **Split view** — Drag the divider between table/board and calendar to size each pane however you like.

## Pro tips
- Use the Area column to group work by client, campaign or workstream.
- Block calendar time in 30-minute slots during launch windows — that’s when things slip.
