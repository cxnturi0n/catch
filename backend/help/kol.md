# KOL Tracker

Tracking ambassadors and influencers

Your ambassador CRM. Every KOL relationship lives here, status, reach, last contact, notes.

## How to use it
1. Add KOLs as you onboard them, with their channel and reach.
2. Update status after each campaign wraps.
3. Sort by reach to prioritize outreach for upcoming campaigns.

## Status explained
- **Active**, Currently running campaigns with this KOL.
- **Pending**, In negotiation or onboarding, not yet confirmed.
- **Inactive**, Past relationship, not currently engaged.

## Pro tips
- Add notes after every interaction, memory fades fast across dozens of KOLs.
- Track reach carefully, vanity metrics vs. real engagement differ widely in Web3.
