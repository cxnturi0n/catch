# Plans and quotas

| Plan | Workspaces | Moderators | AI status updates per day | AI-narrated reports per month | Chat messages per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 | 3 | 10 | 4 | 20 |
| Pro | 2 | 10 | 50 | 30 | 100 |
| Agency | 8 | 40 | 200 | 150 | 400 |
| Enterprise | unlimited | unlimited | 1000 | 1000 | 2000 |

- Quotas are enforced on the server. When the AI report quota is reached, reports are still generated with rule-based narrative; the numbers never depend on the AI.
- Chat message quotas count per user per day; each message may use a limited number of data tools.
- Upgrade from the plan card in the sidebar.
